These "transformations" are essentially purifying model outputs that may have excess data
or recording data I want to store somewhere or maybe append more data.

For example, T1 will take the users input and generate the wildtype fasta
T2 will take the output from the ubiquitination predictor and mutant list

These check points have little to do with practical form and everything to do with modulation!
By creating cinch points where data will flow through, any model can attached in between T1 and T2. 
Likewise, there can be multiple variations of a transformation, such as a transfromation that takes 
the site list and turns it into a list of mutants. The mutations can be made singuarly, combinatorialy,
with one amino, multiple, all of them, so I want to accomadate modulating that as well.
